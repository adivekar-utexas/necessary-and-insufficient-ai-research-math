CODE OF CONDUCT FOR PARTICIPATION 
IN “AI MATH FROM SCRATCH” PROJECT

This Agreement pertains to the “AI Math From Scratch” textbook project, which provides students a learning opportunity related to the fields of artificial intelligence and mathematics. 
Parties to this Agreement
The parties to the Agreement are: 
(1)	"Organizer": Mr. Abhishek Divekar (email: adivekar@utexas.edu). The founder of the open-source "AI Math From Scratch" textbook project that is available at GitHub.com
(2)	Contributor to the Project (or "Contributor"): Refers to the individual who will be contributing to the project. 
(3)	"Parent/Guardian": Refers to the Parent/Guardian acting on behalf of and representing the Contributor if the Contributor is a Minor. 

Background
(A)	The "AI Math From Scratch" textbook project (the "Project") is an open-source, educational work focused on learning and development in artificial intelligence and mathematics.
(B)	The Project aims to provide a learning opportunity to the Contributor to learn and contribute to the development of the open-source textbook related to artificial intelligence and mathematics.
1.	Definitions
1.1.	Project means the "AI Math From Scratch" textbook project, an open-source, educational work focused on artificial intelligence and mathematics.
1.2.	Contributions means the text, code, figures, diagrams, multimedia content and other materials contributed by the Contributor to the Project.
1.3.	LLM tools means public Large Language Model tools, such as ChatGPT, that the Contributor may use within either self-provisioned by the Contributor or via a moderated workspace for the Project, with all prompts and outputs logged for safety and administrative purposes by the Organizer.
1.4.	Code of Conduct means the set of rules and guidelines established by the Organizer that the Contributor  agrees to follow while participating in the Project.
1.5.	Effective Date is the date on which this Agreement becomes effective. It will be the date on which the Contributor submits their first Contribution to the Project.
2.	Detailed Code of Conduct for Participation in the Project
The detailed Code of Conduct specified for Participation in the Project is as follows:
2.1.	Project Purpose: The purpose of this project is to provide a learning opportunity to the Contributor related to the fields of artificial intelligence and mathematics.
2.2.	Voluntary Participation: The Contributor's participation in the Project is purely for learning purposes on a voluntary, remote, and unpaid basis and the Contributor shall not be entitled to any payment or guaranteed non-monetary rewards for their Contributions on the Project. 
2.3.	Fees Payable to Organizer: No fees are payable by the Contributor or the Parent/Guardian (if the Contributor is a Minor) to the Organizer for the Contributor's participation in the Project.
2.4.	Contribution Protocols: The Contributor is permitted to use public Large Language Model (LLM) tools, such as ChatGPT, either self-provisioned by the Contributor, or within a moderated workspace provided by the Organizer, for their Contributions. If provided a moderated workspace, all prompts and outputs generated by the Contributor using such LLM tools may be logged for safety and administrative purposes.
2.5.	Originality of Contributions: The Contributor warrants that their Contributions are original and do not infringe upon any third-party intellectual property rights.
2.6.	Organizer's Right to Include, Edit and Delete Contributions: The Organizer shall have full discretion in including, editing, modifying or deleting the contributions as provided by the Contributor.
2.7.	Intellectual Property Rights: The Organizer is hereby granted by the Contributor and the Parent/Guardian (in case the Contributor is a minor), a perpetual, irrevocable, royalty-free license to use, reproduce, modify, and distribute the Contributions under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0) license.
2.8.	Publishing of Contributions: The Contributions made by the Contributor shall be published worldwide under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 (CC-BY 4.0) license, and the Contributor's name may be listed in the acknowledgments of the Project at the discretion of the Organizer.
2.9.	Recognition of Contributions made by the Contributor: The Organizer shall have the sole discretion to determine the specific content to be recognised and the recognition to be provided to the Contributor for the same. This may include, but is not limited to, certificates or letters of recommendation.
2.10.	Storage of Personal Data: Outside of Contributions, the Organizer may collect and store the following personal data of the Contributor for the sole purpose of Project administration:
(a)	Name of Contributor and of Parent/Guardian (if the Contributor is a minor).
(b)	GitHub handle.
(c)	Email address and contact details.
(d)	Revision history of the Contributor's Contributions and any other information as relevant.
2.11.	Termination of the Agreement by Contributor: The Contributor or the Parent/Guardian (in case the Contributor is a minor) shall have the right to terminate this Agreement and withdraw the Contributor's participation in the Project at any time by providing at least 1 week's written or email notice to the Organizer. However, all Contributions made by the Contributor up to such time will be retained or removed from the Project at the sole discretion of the Organizer.
2.12.	Termination of the Agreement by Organizer: The Organizer reserves the right to terminate this Agreement at any time for any reason whatsoever as per the needs of the Project by providing at least 1 week's written or email notice to the Contributor and/or the Parent/Guardian as applicable.
2.13.	Project Closure and Completion: Unless terminated as outlined above, the "Project Closure Date" or "Completion Date" will be three years from the "Effective Date" of the Agreement.
2.14.	Confidentiality: The Contributor shall maintain strict confidentiality related to any information specifically identified by the Organizer as "Confidential" or "Secret".
2.15.	Limitation of Liability: The Organizer shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential, or incidental damages arising out of or in connection with the Contributor's participation in the Project.
2.16.	Indemnification: The Contributor and the Parent/Guardian (in case the Contributor is a minor) shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the Organizer against any and all claims, liabilities, damages, losses, costs, and expenses arising out of or in connection with the Contributor's participation in the Project or any breach of this Agreement by the Contributor. 
2.17.	Governing Law and Jurisdiction: This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India. Any disputes and legal proceedings arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Mumbai, India. 
2.18.	Amendments: The Organizer reserves the right to modify, amend or waiver any provision of this Agreement at any time. Any such modifications or amendments shall be effective upon written notice to the Contributor and/or the Parent/Guardian (if applicable). The Contributor's continued participation in the Project after such notice shall constitute acceptance of the modified terms.
2.19.	Severability: If any provision or part of this Agreement is held to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.
